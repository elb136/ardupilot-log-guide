Reading ArduPilot Logs

Message and parameter names follow Copter 4.5/4.6. A few position-controller and filter names change between major versions, so confirm against your firmware's parameter list before writing values.

The first five minutes with a log

Read the story before you read the sensors. Text, errors and mode changes give you a timestamp; then you zoom ±10 s around it and check systems in a fixed order.

  1. Get the right file. Use the DataFlash .BIN from the SD card or MAVFTP, not the .tlog (telemetry is low-rate and drops packets). Check LOG_BITMASK includes ATTITUDE, RCOUT, PID, IMU, MOTBATT; the default covers this. For filter work, enable batch or raw IMU logging (see Notch/FFT).
  2. Read MSG. Every warning the GCS showed is here with a timestamp: Potential Thrust Loss, Yaw Imbalance, Vibration compensation ON, EKF3 IMU1 switched, GPS Glitch, Crash: Disarming. Also the firmware version and board info at the top.
  3. Read ERR and EV. ERR gives subsystem + code (table below). EV gives armed (10), disarmed (11), land complete (18) and similar events. ECode 0 almost always means "cleared".
  4. Read MODE. Mode and Rsn (reason): 1 = pilot RC, 2 = GCS, 3 = radio failsafe, 4 = battery failsafe, 5 = GCS failsafe, 6 = EKF failsafe. A mode change you didn't command is your timestamp.
  5. Then systems, in this order: VIBE → RCOU/MOTB → BAT/POWR → ATT/RATE → XKF4/GPS/MAG → PM. Vibration and power problems cause symptoms in everything after them, so rule them out first.
  6. Work backwards from the event. Physical failures show up in RCOU first (a motor saturates), then in ATT (attitude error grows), then in the EKF and position. The earliest abnormal signal is usually the cause.

Vibration and tuning

VIBEIMU

Vibration

Symptoms: altitude climbs or drops in AltHold/Loiter, toilet-bowling, "Vibration compensation ON", hot motors, mushy position hold.

Where to look

VIBE.VibeX/Y/Z
Accel vibration, m/s². Z matters most because it feeds altitude.
VIBE.Clip
Count of accel samples that hit the sensor range. Should stay flat. Any rise means the accel saturated.
VIBE.IMU
Instance. Compare IMUs; a single bad one points to mounting, not props.
IMU.AccZ
Raw view. Hover should sit near −9.8 with a narrow band.
<15 great15–30 OK30–60 problems>60 position/alt hold will fail

How to react

  • All axes high, Clip rising: mechanical first. Balance or replace props, check bent shafts and bearings, tighten the frame, soft-mount the FC.
  • Only Z high: usually props or a flexing arm/stack. On whoops, check the flight controller isn't touching the canopy.
  • One IMU bad, others fine: that IMU's mounting or a damaged sensor. Use EK3_IMU_MASK to drop it while you fix it.
  • High only at high throttle: resonance in the frame. Check the FFT section and add a harmonic notch.

ISBH/ISBDFTNESC

Gyro noise and the harmonic notch

Symptoms: hot motors, high D-term noise, can't raise PIDs without oscillation, jitter at specific throttle.

Where to look

Batch sampler
Set INS_LOG_BAT_MASK=1 and INS_LOG_BAT_OPT=4 to log both pre- and post-filter gyro. Fly a 1–2 min hover with some throttle changes.
Filter Review
WebTools → Filter Review. Load the log, you get this spectrum, then edit notch params live to see the effect.
ESC.RPM
If you have BDShot/ESC telemetry, motor frequency is RPM/60 Hz, and the notch can track it exactly.
RATE.ROut / POut
Fuzzy outputs mean noise is getting through to the motors.

How to react

  • Peak at motor fundamental + harmonics: enable the harmonic notch. With ESC telemetry use INS_HNTCH_MODE=3; otherwise throttle-based (1) with INS_HNTCH_REF = hover throttle.
  • Post-filter still shows the peak: the notch is in the wrong place. Re-check FREQ/REF, or the notch isn't tracking (wrong MODE, no RPM data).
  • Separate peaks per motor: use per-motor notches (INS_HNTCH_OPTS bit 1) with ESC RPM.
  • Broad noise floor, no peak: mechanical. Fix props and mounting; notches only remove narrow peaks.

RATEPIDR/PIDP/PIDYATT

PID tuning: desired vs actual

Symptoms: wobble on stops, bounce-back after flips, sluggish response, oscillation at high throttle or in descent.

Where to look

RATE.RDes vs R
Desired vs actual roll rate (deg/s). Same for PDes/P, YDes/Y. This is the main tuning plot.
RATE.ROut
Controller output. Should be smooth; saturating at ±1 means out of authority.
PIDR.P/I/D/FF
Term contributions. Large D swinging fast is noise; large I wandering means CG or a motor offset.
ATT.DesRoll vs Roll
Angle level. Use after rates look good.

How to react

  • Fast, sustained oscillation (fuzzy band): P or D too high, or noise. Lower ATC_RAT_RLL_D first, then P by 10–20%. Check FFT before blaming PIDs.
  • Big overshoot, slow ringing: P too high for the D, or I too high. Raise D slightly or lower P.
  • Actual lags desired with no overshoot: too soft. Raise P (and I to match), or run AutoTune.
  • Oscillates only at high throttle: thrust curve. Raise MOT_THST_EXPO, or lower P/D; check MOT_BAT_VOLT_MAX/MIN are set.
  • Oscillates only in fast descent: prop wash. Usually accept it, or slightly raise D.
  • PIDY.I parked far from zero: motors tilted or one weaker; the yaw I is holding a constant offset.

ATTERR 12RCOU

Crash or loss of control

Symptoms: "Crash: Disarming", sudden flip, log ends mid-air, ERR subsystem 12.

Where to look

ATT.DesRoll vs Roll
Divergence (actual runs away while desired stays put) means the airframe could not follow.
RCOU.C1–C4
Which motor saturated just before. This names the failed corner.
BAT / POWR
Voltage collapse → brownout. A log that just stops with no ERR, at speed, is often a power loss.
CTUN.ThO
Throttle output at the moment. 100% means it was already out of headroom.

How to react

  • One motor maxed, attitude diverges toward that corner: motor, ESC, or prop failure on that corner. Inspect and bench-test that ESC/motor.
  • All motors maxed, roll/pitch drift: overweight or battery sag; no authority left. See Motors and Battery.
  • Attitude fine, then log stops: power loss or brownout. Check POWR.Vcc trend, BEC, battery connector.
  • Desired itself went wild: it was commanded. Look at RCIN and MODE (bad nav, flyaway from EKF/GPS, or pilot input).

Power and propulsion

RCOUMOTBCTUN

Motor outputs, thrust loss, yaw imbalance

Symptoms: "Potential Thrust Loss (n)", "Yaw Imbalance x%", drifting yaw, a corner that sags, motors different temperatures.

Where to look

RCOU.C1–C4
PWM to each motor. In hover they should sit close together, mid-range.
MOTB.ThLimit
Throttle limit applied (e.g. by battery or saturation).
MOTB.FailFlags
Motor failure / thrust boost flags.
CTUN.ThO / ThH
Throttle out and learned hover throttle.

Quad X order: 1 front-right (CCW), 2 rear-left (CCW), 3 front-left (CW), 4 rear-right (CW). 1↔2 and 3↔4 are diagonals.

How to react

  • One motor pinned at max, its diagonal at min: that motor produces less thrust than commanded. Check prop, motor bearings, ESC, cracked prop. Also "Thrust Loss" if it lasts.
  • CW pair high, CCW pair low (or reverse): yaw imbalance. Twisted arm or tilted motor, mismatched props, or one weak motor. Small offsets (<50 µs) are normal.
  • Front pair consistently higher than rear: CG forward. Move the battery.
  • All high in hover (ThH > 0.6): overweight or underpropped. Little headroom left for control.
  • ThH < 0.15: overpowered, resolution poor at hover. Consider lower KV or smaller props; set MOT_SPIN_MIN carefully.

MOT_SPIN_MINLowest throttle in flight. Must be above ARM and keep motors reliably spinning.0.12–0.15

BATERR 6

Battery sag and false failsafes

Symptoms: battery failsafe triggers on a punch with plenty of capacity left, power drops late in the flight, RTL earlier than expected.

Where to look

BAT.Volt
Measured voltage under load.
BAT.VoltR
Resting voltage estimate (sag-compensated, uses the internal resistance estimate).
BAT.Curr / CurrTot
Current and mAh used. Compare CurrTot to what you actually put back on the charger to validate the sensor.
BAT.Res
Estimated internal resistance. Rising across flights = pack aging.

How to react

  • Volt dips under BATT_LOW_VOLT only on punches, VoltR stays above: false trigger. Set BATT_FS_VOLTSRC=1 so the failsafe uses sag-compensated voltage.
  • Dips >0.3 V per cell under load: pack weak, too small (C-rating), or a bad connector/thin lead. Measure lead temperature.
  • CurrTot doesn't match recharge mAh: recalibrate BATT_AMP_PERVLT (and BATT_AMP_OFFSET).
  • Handling changes as the pack drains: set MOT_BAT_VOLT_MAX/MIN so the PIDs are compensated for voltage.

BATT_LOW_VOLTLow failsafe threshold.14.0 (3.5/cell)
BATT_CRT_VOLTCritical threshold.13.6 (3.4/cell)
BATT_FS_VOLTSRC0 = raw, 1 = sag-compensated.1
BATT_LOW_TIMERSeconds below threshold before triggering.10
BATT_FS_LOW_ACTAction: 1 land, 2 RTL, 3 SmartRTL…2
BATT_LOW_MAHCapacity-based trigger (more reliable than voltage if the sensor is calibrated).~20% of pack

POWR

Flight controller power

Symptoms: random reboots, log ends with no error, sensor glitches when servos/VTX/camera switch, compass jumping.

Where to look

POWR.Vcc
FC 5 V rail. Should be flat, ±0.1 V.
POWR.VServo
Servo rail voltage (if the board measures it).
POWR.Flags
Power source bits and USB connected. Changing flags in flight = a source dropped out.

How to react

  • Dips synchronous with servo or VTX activity: shared BEC is overloaded. Give servos/VTX their own BEC, add a low-ESR capacitor.
  • Slow downward drift: BEC overheating or current-limiting.
  • Vcc fine but log ends abruptly: check the battery lead and XT connector; look at BAT.Volt just before the end.

Navigation and estimation

MAGXKF4.SMBAT.Curr

Compass interference

Symptoms: toilet-bowling in Loiter, heading wrong after a punch, "EKF3 yaw reset", circling that grows with throttle.

Where to look

|MAG|
Field length: sqrt(MagX²+MagY²+MagZ²). Should be nearly constant during a flight (local field, roughly 400–550 mGauss depending on location).
BAT.Curr
Overlay with |MAG|. Correlation = power-wire interference.
MAG vs MAG2
Compare instances. External (on a mast) should be steady; internal one usually tracks current.
XKF4.SM
Mag innovation test ratio. Above 1 = EKF rejecting the compass.

How to react

  • |MAG| moves >10–20% with current: move the compass away from power leads and ESCs, twist battery leads. Then run compassmot or MAGFit with COMPASS_MOTCT=2.
  • Internal compass bad, external fine: disable the internal one with COMPASS_USE2=0 (or whichever instance).
  • Constant heading offset vs GPS track in straight flight: wrong COMPASS_ORIENT or bad calibration. Recalibrate; MAGFit can solve it from a flight log.
  • Can't fix it: run without a compass using GSF yaw (COMPASS_ENABLE=0, EK3_SRC1_YAW=8). Needs GPS and some forward motion to align.

GPSGPAERR 11

GPS quality and interference

Symptoms: "GPS Glitch", Loiter drifts or jumps, can't arm in Loiter, position wanders while stationary.

Where to look

GPS.NSats
Satellites used. Want >12 for multi-GNSS.
GPS.HDop
Horizontal dilution. <1.0 good, >1.4 weak.
GPA.HAcc / SAcc
Receiver's own accuracy estimates (m, m/s). Better than HDop on modern receivers.
GPA.Delta
Interval between updates, ms. Jitter or gaps = link or CPU problem.
XKF4.SP / SV
EKF position/velocity test ratios. Shows whether the EKF believes the GPS.

How to react

  • NSats drops when something powers on: RF interference. VTX harmonics, USB 3, cameras and SD cards are common. Move the GPS up and away, shield, or change VTX channel.
  • Steady low NSats: enable more constellations (GPS_GNSS_MODE), check antenna has sky view.
  • Position jumps but NSats OK: multipath. Fly away from buildings; raise EK3_GLITCH_RAD only if you understand the risk.
  • Position leans during fast turns: antenna offset not set. Fill in GPS_POS1_X/Y/Z.

EKF health

Symptoms: "EKF variance", EKF failsafe → Land, lane switches, "EKF3 IMU0 forced reset", flyaway.

Where to look

XKF4.SV / SP / SH / SM
Test ratios for velocity, horizontal position, height, magnetometer. Below 1 means the measurement was accepted. Each core logs its own (C field).
XKF3.I*
Innovations: measurement minus prediction. A persistent bias shows which sensor disagrees.
XKF4.SS
Solution status bitmask.
ERR 24 / MSG
Which lane (IMU) became primary and when.
<0.5 healthy0.5–1 watch>1 rejected

How to react

  • SM climbs with throttle: compass interference. See Compass.
  • SV and SP spike together: GPS glitch. See GPS.
  • SH high: baro vs GPS/accel height disagree. Prop wash on baro (foam it), or Z vibration.
  • Frequent lane switches: one IMU disagrees. Compare VIBE per IMU and IMU temperatures.
  • EKF failsafe: it triggers when two of compass, position, velocity variances exceed FS_EKF_THRESH. Fix the sensor; don't just raise the threshold.

Altitude hold

Symptoms: bouncing in AltHold, climbs on punches, drops in fast forward flight, slow to stop climbs.

Where to look

CTUN.DAlt vs Alt
Desired vs estimated altitude.
CTUN.BAlt
Raw baro altitude. Compare to Alt to see baro errors.
CTUN.DCRt vs CRt
Desired vs actual climb rate.
CTUN.ThO / ThH
Throttle out and hover estimate. ThH should converge and stay stable.

How to react

  • Slow bounce (0.3–1 Hz): vertical controller too aggressive. Lower PSC_ACCZ_P and PSC_ACCZ_I together (keep I = 2×P).
  • Alt drifts while DAlt is constant, VibeZ high: vibration. Fix VIBE first.
  • BAlt dips in fast forward flight: static pressure effect on the baro. Cover the baro with open-cell foam, shield from airflow.
  • Jump at takeoff/landing only: ground effect on baro; normal, ArduPilot compensates.
  • ThH wrong or not learning: MOT_HOVER_LEARN=2 and hover a minute.

PSCNPSCENTUNATT

Position control in Loiter

Symptoms: toilet-bowling, drifting, back-and-forth wobble while holding, overshoot when you let go of the sticks, slow or jerky braking.

Where to look

PSCN.TPN vs PN
Target vs actual North position (m). PSCE.TPE vs PE for East. The difference is position error.
PSCN.TVN vs VN
Target vs actual velocity (m/s). The velocity loop is where most Loiter tuning happens.
PSCN.TAN vs AN
Target vs actual acceleration. Large mismatch = the attitude loop can't deliver the requested tilt.
ATT.DesRoll/Pitch
Lean angles the position controller asks for. Pegged at LOIT_ANG_MAX means it's out of authority (wind, too heavy).

Plot error directly in MAVExplorer: graph PSCN.PN-PSCN.TPN PSCE.PE-PSCE.TPE. A shape that's two sines 90° apart, growing, is a circle. The same sine on one axis only is a tuning oscillation.

<0.5 m hover error0.5–2 m driftinggrowing circles

How to react

Rule out sensors before tuning. Loiter sits on top of attitude, EKF, compass and GPS; it's the last thing to tune.

  • Circles that grow (toilet bowl), N and E errors 90° out of phase: heading is wrong. Compass interference or bad orientation. Go to Compass; check XKF4.SM. Not a PID problem.
  • Steady oscillation on one axis, constant amplitude: velocity loop too aggressive. Lower PSC_VELXY_P 20–30%, and PSC_VELXY_D if set.
  • Slow drift that eventually comes back: too soft. Raise PSC_VELXY_I (wind rejection), then PSC_POSXY_P.
  • Steady offset in wind: I too low, or DesRoll/Pitch pegged at LOIT_ANG_MAX.
  • Overshoot after releasing sticks: braking. Raise LOIT_BRK_ACCEL or lower LOIT_BRK_DELAY.
  • Position jumps with no tracking issue: the estimate jumped, not the copter. Check GPS and XKF4.SP at that time.
  • Actual acceleration lags target: the attitude/rate loop is too soft. Fix PID tuning first.

OFRFNDXKF5

Optical flow (non-GPS Loiter)

Symptoms: drifts in Loiter indoors, speeds up and runs away, position hold gets worse with height, fine over carpet and bad over smooth floor.

Where to look

OF.flowX vs bodyX
Flow rate vs gyro rate (rad/s), X axis. Same for flowY/bodyY. This is the calibration check: rock the copter and they should overlap.
OF.Qual
Surface quality 0–255. Want it consistently above ~100. Low over shiny/plain floors or in dim light.
RFND.Dist
Rangefinder height. Flow velocity = flow rate × height, so a bad rangefinder makes flow velocity wrong.
XKF5.FIX / FIY
EKF flow innovations. Should be small and centered on zero.
XKF5.HAGL
EKF height above ground. Compare to RFND.Dist.

How to react

  • flowX amplitude larger or smaller than bodyX: scale error. Adjust FLOW_FXSCALER / FLOW_FYSCALER (parts per thousand) until they match, or use the in-flight calibration aux switch (RCx_OPTION 158).
  • flowX looks like bodyX inverted or mixed with Y: orientation wrong. Fix FLOW_ORIENT_YAW. A sign error makes Loiter run away instead of hold.
  • Drift only during yaw: sensor offset from CG. Set FLOW_POS_X/Y/Z.
  • Qual drops and position wanders: surface texture or lighting. Add texture, more light; flow can't fix that in params.
  • Worse with height: rangefinder noisy or out of range, or flow rate exceeding EK3_MAX_FLOW. Keep within the sensor's rated height.

Links and system

RCINRSSIERR 2/5

RC link and radio failsafe

Symptoms: unexpected RTL/Land with MODE.Rsn = 3, control freezes, "Radio Failsafe".

Where to look

RCIN.C3
Throttle input (or your throttle channel). PWM receivers drop it below FS_THR_VALUE on loss.
RSSI.RXRSSI
Link quality/RSSI as reported. Slope down before a failsafe = range; a cliff = antenna, connector, or power.
ERR 2 / ERR 5
Radio frames late; radio failsafe triggered/cleared.
MODE.Rsn
3 = mode changed by radio failsafe.

With CRSF/ELRS/SBUS the receiver usually stops sending frames on loss; RCIN then freezes at its last value and the FC detects the timeout (ERR 2). Don't expect the throttle dip shown in the graph with those.

How to react

  • RSSI fades gradually first: range or antenna orientation. Check RX antenna placement and TX power.
  • Short dropouts at high throttle: RX antenna near carbon or power wires, or RX brownout.
  • Failsafe while RSSI looked fine: UART framing or wiring. Check SERIALn_PROTOCOL=23 and baud.

PMDSF

CPU load and logging gaps

Symptoms: gaps in the log, jerky control, "Internal errors", odd behaviour after enabling high-rate logging, Lua scripts, or many peripherals.

Where to look

PM.Load
CPU load, in tenths of a percent (300 = 30%).
PM.NLon / NLoop
Long loops vs total loops. NLon should be a tiny fraction.
PM.MaxT
Longest loop, µs. At 400 Hz the budget is 2500 µs.
DSF.Dp
Dropped log messages. Nonzero means slow SD card or too much logging.
PM.IntE
Internal error bitmask. Any nonzero value is worth reporting.

How to react

  • Load high after enabling raw IMU / batch logging: expected while tuning; turn it off afterward.
  • DSF.Dp rising: replace SD card (fast, small, freshly formatted), trim LOG_BITMASK.
  • F4/F7 board pegged with many features: reduce SCHED_LOOP_RATE only as last resort; disable unused scripts and peripherals first.
